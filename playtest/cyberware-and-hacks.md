# Cyberware & Hacks

## Cyberware

Each individual **Cyberware** installed reduces your **Max CTR** by 1. You can install multiple of the same cyberware. Installed cyberware does not occupy an inventory slot.

Most cyberware has passive effects, providing a constant benefit to characters. Some cyberware have **Triggers** for active effects, at the cost of taking Stress. As a free action, you can trigger a bonus effect by taking stress equal to a roll of the associated die. If duration is unclear, roll the trigger die to determine number of rounds an effect lasts.

See [Marketplace > Cyberware](/playtest/marketplace/cyberware) for a list of available cyberware.


## Hacks

A **Cyberdeck** must be held to deploy a **Hack**. Each **Hack** occupies 1 inventory slot.

Hacks are resolved through the following steps:
1. All hackers with the same target roll stress dice.
2. Take the value from the highest result.
3. All hackers take stress equal to that value, subtracting their individual Buffer.
4. Subtract the target's Buffer from the value, dealing stress to the target equal to the remaining total.
5. If the target takes at least 1 stress, apply the effects from all hacks used.

If duration is unclear, roll the hack die to determine number of rounds an effect lasts.

See [Marketplace > Hacks](/playtest/marketplace/hacks) for a list of available hacks.
