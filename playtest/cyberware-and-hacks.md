# Cyberware & Hacks

## Cyberware

Each individual **Cyberware** installed reduces your **Max CTR** by 1. You can install multiple of the same cyberware. Installed cyberware does not occupy an inventory slot.

As a free action, you can **Trigger** a bonus effect by taking stress equal to a roll of the associated die. If duration is unclear, roll the trigger die to determine number of rounds an effect lasts.

See [Marketplace > Cyberware](/playtest/marketplace/cyberware) for a list of available cyberware.


## Hacks

A **Cyberdeck** must be used to deploy a **Hack**. A **Hack** module occupies 1 inventory slot.

As an action, you can deploy a **Hack** by taking stress equal to a roll of the associated die, and applying the same stress to your target. The hack is successful if the target takes at least 1 stress after subtracting their Buffer. Hacks are always successful on targets without Buffer. If duration is unclear, roll the hack die to determine number of rounds an effect lasts.

See [Marketplace > Hacks](/playtest/marketplace/hacks) for a list of available hacks.
