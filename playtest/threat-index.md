# Threat Index

## Categories

|        |           |                                                                    |
| ------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **d4** | **Group** | **Threats**                                                        |
| **1**  | Street    | Cyberpsycho, Street Ganger                                         |
| **2**  | Corporate | Corpo Operator, Netrunner                                          |
| **3**  | Robotic   | Aero-Drone, Automated Turret, Industrial Servitor, Metalhead Hound |
| **4**  | Military  | Chimera Tank                                                       |

## Aero-Drone

4 Stamina, 1 Armor, 4 BOD, 16 AGL, 10 CTR, micro-laser (d4)

- Small, cheap corporate surveillance drones equipped with lethal deterrents.

## Automated Turret

0 Stamina, 1 Armor, 10 BOD, 10 CTR, heavy chaingun (d10, _blast_)

- Stationary defence.

## Chimera Tank

15 Stamina, 3 Armor, 16 BOD, 8 AGL, 10 CTR, thermal beam (d12, _blast_), _hardened_

- A walking tank designed for pacifying entire city blocks.
- **Recharge Protocol**: Can spend a full turn recovering 2d6 Stamina

## Corpo Operator

8 Stamina, 1 Armor, 1 Buffer, 12 BOD, 14 AGL, 10 CTR, assault rifle (d8, _autofire_, _suppress_)

- Highly trained corporate hitmen.

## Cyberpsycho

12 Stamina, 2 Armor, 1 Buffer, 14 BOD, 14 AGL, 4 CTR, arm blades (d8+d8), redline (d6, _cyberware_) or speedware (d8, _cyberware_)

- Individuals who have pushed their nervous system past the breaking point with heavy cyberware. Highly unpredictable and violent.
- **Critical Damage**: Their attacks cause massive bleeding. Targets are _deprived_ from blood loss.


## Industrial Servitor

4 Stamina, 1 Armor, 8 BOD, 4 AGL, 6 CTR, hydraulic clamps (d10)

- Repurposed dock workers or factory lifters. Slow, hulking, and incredibly dangerous in close quarters.
- **Slow Processing**: their attacks against targets that have recently moved are _impaired_.


## Metalhead Hound

6 Stamina, 1 Armor, 8 BOD, 14 AGL, 6 CTR, titanium jaws (d8)

- Quadrupedal security robots used for perimeter defense. Fast rushers.
- Will focus all attacks on the first person to deal damage to them, and do not switch targets.

## Netrunner

4 Stamina, 2 Buffer, 8 BOD, 11 AGL, 14 CTR, optic reset (d4), overheat (d6)

- Fragile in meat-space but deadly if they have access to the local subnet.

## Street Ganger

3 Stamina, 10 BOD, 12 AGL, 9 CTR, metal pipe (d6) or pistol (d6)

- Low-level syndicate thugs or desperate locals. Rarely fight to the death unless cornered.
- **Critical Damage**: Target is mugged in the scuffle, dropping a random item (melee only).
