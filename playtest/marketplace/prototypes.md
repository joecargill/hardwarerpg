# Prototypes

> Prototypes are the result of immeasurable capital investment in research and development, and are impossible to obtain through normal means. Corporations will ruthlessly pursue missing prototypes.

## Flatline Engram

A digitized recording of the personality of a famous dead hacker. Assist with their expertise when connected to your cyberdeck.

- When using Hacks, roll the stress die twice. Take the lower result for yourself, and the higher result for your target.

## Experimental Speedware

_Cyberware_. Military-grade cyberware that is highly demanding of the user. Visibly attached to the exterior of the spine, making it difficult to cover without purpose-made clothing or armor.

- Always go first in combat, with priority over standard Speedware. 
- **Trigger** (d8) to take both extra movement and action.

## Chrome Compressor

_Cyberware_. Brain-stem signal controller that is able to reduce the neural load caused by cyberware implants, allowing for heavier augmentation than usually possible.

- Ignore Max CTR cost of this installed cyberware, and up to three other cyberware.

## Personality Cloak

_Cyberware_. Full-body hologram tech able to alter skin colour, facial features, voice, and all other features to look and sound identical to target for which you have a DNA sample. Limited by physical features such as height and other obvious body proportions.

- Disabled upon taking damage.
