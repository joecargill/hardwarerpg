# Research Projects

> Prototypes are the result of immeasurable capital investment in research and development, and are impossible to obtain through normal means. Corporations will ruthlessly pursue missing prototypes.

## Flatline Engram

A digitized recording of the personality of a famous dead hacker. Assist with their expertise when connected to your cyberdeck.

- When using Hacks roll the stress die twice, taking the lower result for yourself and the higher result for your target.

## Experimental Speedware

Military-grade cyberware that is highly demanding of the user. Visibly attached to the exterior of the spine, making it difficult to cover without purpose-made clothing or armor.

- Always go first in combat, with priority over standard Speedware. 
- **Trigger** (d8, _free action_) to take both extra movement and action.

## Chrome Compressor

Brain-stem signal controller that is able to reduce the neural load caused by cyberware implants, allowing for heavier augmentation than usually possible.

- Ignore Max CTR cost of up to three installed cyberware.
