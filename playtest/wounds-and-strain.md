# Wounds & Strain

## Wounds

When you fail a **Critical Damage** save after taking BOD damage, you gain a **Wound** in an inventory slot. If your inventory is full, you must drop an item.

Use the amount of total damage taken (after Armor and Cover) to determine the wound _severity_ and its effects on the table below.

### Wound table

Roll 1d8 for location if required. 

**1**: Neck, **2**: Hands, **3**: Eye, **4**: Chest, **5**: Leg, **6**: Arm, **7**: Rib, **8**: Head.

|                          |                                                                             |                   |                 |                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| **Severity**             |                                                                             | **Gunshot**       | **Cutting**     | **Crushing**      |
| **1-2**<br>*Superficial* | Flesh wound.                                                                | Graze             | Shallow cut     | Bruise            |
| **3-4**<br>*Minor*       | _Impaired_ until you receive first aid.                                     | Clean through     | Deep laceration | Hairline fracture |
| **5-6**<br>*Serious*     | _Dying_ until stabilised.                                                   | Lodged projectile | Severed muscle  | Internal bleeding |
| **7-8**<br>*Major*       | _Dying_. _Deprived_ until you rest for a few hours.                         | Shattered bone    | Cut to the bone | Broken limb       |
| **9-10**<br>*Critical*   | _Dying_. _Deprived_. A limb is missing or useless.                          | Destroyed limb    | Severed limb    | Mangled limb      |
| **11-12**<br>*Lethal*    | _Dying_. _Deprived_. You die the next time you fail a Critical Damage save. | Massive hole      | Eviscerated     | Shattered skull   |

### Wound recovery

 Your wounds heal, but they leave lasting scars. Your experience leaves you with permanent reminders of your brush with death, and you find yourself grown 
 
 After you have fully recovered and you have no Wounds remaining in your inventory, apply only the result from the most severe Wound received.


|               |                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Superficial* | No effect                                                                                                                                             |
| *Minor*       | Roll 1d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                                               |
| *Serious*     | Roll 1d6; 1-3 BOD, 4-6 AGL. Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max attribute, increase it by 1. If you roll multiple ones, reduce it by 1.    |
| *Major*       | Roll 2d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                                               |
| *Critical*    | Roll 1d6; 1-3 BOD, 4-6 AGL. Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max attribute, take the new result. If you roll multiple ones, reduce it by 1. |
| *Lethal*      | Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                                               |




## Strain

When you fail a **Critical Stress** save after taking CTR damage, you gain a **Strain** in an inventory slot. If your inventory is full, you must drop an item.

Use the amount of total stress taken (after Buffer) to determine the strain _severity_ and its effects on the table below. Strain suffered outside of high-pressure situations is always _Superficial_.

### Strain table

|                          |                                                                                                                                                |                  |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------- |
| **Severity**             |                                                                                                                                                | **Stress**       |
| **1-2**<br>*Superficial* | Agitated.                                                                                                                                      | Overstimulated   |
| **3-4**<br>*Minor*       | On your next turn, you cannot use Hacks or Triggers.                                                                                           | Mental latency   |
| **5-6**<br>*Serious*     | When using Hacks and Triggers, roll the die twice taking the higher result for yourself, and the lower result for your target if you have one. | Sensory overload |
| **7-8**<br>*Major*       | _Deprived_ until you rest a few hours.                                                                                                         | Burnout          |
| **9-10**<br>*Critical*   | Out of action, and fully catatonic in one hour without intervention.                                                                           | Dissociation     |
| **11-12**<br>*Lethal*    | You enter a psychotic rage attacking the nearest target with an Edge until stopped.                                                            | Psychotic break  |

### Strain recovery

Your strain dissipates, but it leaves your mind changed. 
 
 After you have fully recovered and have no Strains remaining in your inventory, apply only the result from the most severe Strain received.

|               |                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Superficial* | No effect                                                                                                                                 |
| *Minor*       | Roll 1d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                               |
| *Serious*     | Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max CTR (excluding cyberware), increase it by 1. If you roll multiple ones, reduce it by 1.    |
| *Major*       | Roll 2d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                               |
| *Critical*    | Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max CTR (excluding cyberware), take the new result. If you roll multiple ones, reduce it by 1. |
| *Lethal*      | Roll 3d6; if the total is higher than your max Stamina take the new result.                                                               |
